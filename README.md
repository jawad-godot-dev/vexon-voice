# VEXON Voice Studio

**VEXON Voice Studio** is a free, unlimited AI Text-to-Speech (TTS) and synchronized SRT subtitle generator web application. Convert your text into natural human AI voices in 100+ languages instantly.

## 🚀 Live Demo
Try the application live here: [VEXON Voice Studio](https://jawad-godot-dev.github.io/vexon-voice/)

---

## ✨ Key Features
- **Unlimited Text to Speech:** Convert large blocks of text into natural-sounding neural voices with zero limits.
- **100+ Languages & Accents:** Support for global languages and regional dialects (including Urdu and English).
- **Synchronized SRT Generator:** Automatically generate and download synchronized subtitle files (`.srt`) along with your audio.
- **Instant MP3 Download:** Export your generated voiceovers directly to your device as high-quality MP3 audio files.
- **Completely Free:** No hidden fees, subscriptions, or credit cards required.
